## Summary

- VAE + K-Means: RMSE 1.0788, 0.0028 higher than the baseline (p = 1, better in 0/5 folds) and 0.0543 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 0.000129, better in 0/5 folds).
- AE + GMM: RMSE 1.0727, 0.0034 lower than the baseline (p = 0.153, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0481 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 4.66e-05, better in 0/5 folds).
- AE + FCM: RMSE 1.0263, 0.0497 lower than the baseline (p = 3.5e-05, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0018 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 0.762, better in 1/5 folds).
- TF-IDF graph + AE + K-Means: RMSE 1.0777, 0.0016 higher than the baseline (p = 1, better in 1/5 folds) and 0.0531 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 5.15e-05, better in 0/5 folds).
- VAE + GMM: RMSE 1.0657, 0.0103 lower than the baseline (p = 0.0163, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0411 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 0.000275, better in 0/5 folds).
- VAE + FCM: RMSE 1.0269, 0.0491 lower than the baseline (p = 7.81e-05, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0023 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 0.762, better in 2/5 folds).
- VAE + GMM + TF-IDF: RMSE 1.0661, 0.0099 lower than the baseline (p = 0.007, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0415 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 8.61e-06, better in 0/5 folds).
- VAE + FCM + TF-IDF: RMSE 1.0255, 0.0505 lower than the baseline (p = 0.000129, better in 5/5 folds) and 0.0009 higher than GHRS with K = 1 (p = 0.762, better in 2/5 folds).

Variance: S^2 over folds is lower than the baseline's for 1 of 8 variants and the spread over seeds for 1 of 8. 5 folds are too few to tell such differences in variance apart from noise.

With membership-weighted cluster means, softer memberships pull every prediction towards the K = 1 model. Compare each soft variant with its argmax control (same partition, hard labels) and with the K = 1 reference before attributing a gain to the clustering itself.
